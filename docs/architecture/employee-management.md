# Employee Management

# Model
### Employee
- `id` --- PK --- auto-generated by Django
- `first_name`
- `last_name`
- `email`
- Working status:
  - `working_status`:
    - 'active' --- currently working
    - 'archived' ---- no longer working
  - `employment_start_date`
  - `employment_end_date`
- Working agreement:
  - `work_days_per_week` --- by default: 5 days (`Arbeitstage/Woche`)
  - `monthly_target_hours` --- by default: 173 hours (`Sollstunden/Monat`)
- `created_at` --- the date and time the record was created
- `updated_at` --- the date and time the record was last updated


### EmployeeYear
- `id` --- PK --- auto-generated by Django
- `employee` --- FK --- references `Employee`
- `year` --- the year of the employee's employment
- Vacation:
  - `annual_allowed_vacation_days` --- by default: 25 days
  - `carried_over_vacation_days`
    - Explanation: **unused vacation days from the previous year that remain available in the new year**
  - `vacation_balance` --- current remaining vacation days
- `created_at` --- the date and time the record was created
- `updated_at` --- the date and time the record was last updated

### EmployeeMonth
- `id` --- PK --- auto-generated by Django
- `employee` --- FK --- references `Employee`
- `year` --- the year of the employee's employment
- `month` --- the month of the employee's employment

- `opening_hour_account_balance` --- current remaining opening hour account balance carried into the current month from all previous months
  - **Negative value: hours the employee still owes**
  - **Positive value: extra hours the employee has accumulated**
  - **Zero: fully balanced**
  
  - Example:
    ```text
      January: -4 hours
      February: +2 hours
      Opening balance in March: -2 hours
    ```
- `target_working_hours` --- the number of working hours the employee is targeting for this month
  - Calculation: `targeted_working_hours = monthly_target_hours`(173 h)`
- `paid_absence_hours` --- the number of working hours the employee has paid for this month, even though they were not working for the following reasons:
  - Entitiled/Permitted holiday
  - Absent on sick leave
  - The store is closed unplanned ---> employee can't technically work at the store
- `worked_hours` --- the number of working hours the employee has worked this month
- `credited_working_hours` --- the number of the actual working hours for the employee in this month
  - Calculation: `credited_working_hours = worked_hours + paid_absense_hours`
- `hour_difference` --- the number of hours the employee worked this month minus their targeted working hours
  - Calculation: `hour_difference = credited_working_hours - targeted_working_hours`
- `closing_hour_account_balance` --- the closing hour account balance for the employee in this month
  - Calculation: `closing_hour_account_balance = opening_hour_account_balance + hour_difference`

- `opening_vacation_balance` --- the opening vacation balance for the employee in this month
- `vacation_days_used` --- the number of vacation days the employee has used this month
- `closing_vacation_balance` --- the closing vacation balance for the employee in this month
  - Calculation: `closing_vacation_balance = opening_vacation_balance - vacation_days_used`
- `created_at` --- the date and time the employee record was created
- `updated_at` --- the date and time the employee record was last updated
